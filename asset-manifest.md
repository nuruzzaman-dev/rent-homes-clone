## Usage Notes

The page avoids recognizable people, trademarks, watermarks, fabricated customer claims, fabricated property statistics, and invented ratings. The “Stories from people who found home” area is intentionally framed as a placeholder for approved real stories rather than presented as genuine testimonials. The day-in-home section uses a curated sequence of related architectural stills as the prompt permits when a true continuous image sequence is not available; it is explicitly scroll-controlled and does not autoplay.
